Air raid alert hazard forecast

Per oblast: the chance an alert starts soon, or that the current one ends soon, for any alert (yellow or red) and red alerts. Built only from alerts declared before each forecast time.

Red-alert forecasts are not yet better than historical rates (no skill over climatology on validation); treat them as base rates.

Quiet oblasts are coloured by the chance an alert starts within the chosen time; oblasts under alert are coloured blue by the chance it ends within the chosen time. Click an oblast for its full curve.

Forecast vs what happened

Line: the forecast made at each hour that an alert starts within the chosen time (gaps while an alert is already on). Dashed: the climatology baseline. Bands: actual alerts (yellow = any, red = red). A good forecast rises before a band begins.
